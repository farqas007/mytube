MyTube 🔴

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

MyTube is a video browsing and playback website. This page explains, in plain language, what information MyTube handles and how. It describes only the behaviour that is actually implemented in the current site.

Information you provide

When you create an account or sign in, MyTube uses Firebase Authentication, a Google service, with an email address and password. Sign-in is by email and password only; MyTube does not offer "Sign in with Google". The password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never received or stored by MyTube.

When you are signed in, MyTube associates your activity with the user ID that Firebase Authentication issues, so that your own data is shown back to you.

Account email and password

Sign-in is processed by Firebase Authentication, a Google service, using email and password only (no "Sign in with Google"). MyTube does not store your password.

Your comments

Comments you post on MyTube are stored in Cloud Firestore alongside the ID of the account that posted them.

Data stored in your account

MyTube stores the following items in Cloud Firestore under your own user ID, so they are available across your sessions and devices:

Subscriptions

The channels you subscribe to inside MyTube.

Watch history

The videos you have watched through MyTube.

Saved / Watch Later

The videos you have saved.

Liked videos (MyTube library)

Videos saved to your MyTube Liked Library. This is separate from your actual YouTube rating; when you explicitly like a YouTube video, MyTube may add it to this library, but removing a YouTube like/dislike does not automatically remove it from the library.

Playlists

Playlists you create and the videos added to them.

Firestore security rules restrict each of these records so that it can only be read or written by the signed-in account that owns it. MyTube comments are stored in a shared collection so they can be shown publicly, and only the account that created a comment can change or delete it.

Separately from your MyTube account data, if you choose to connect a Google account for YouTube features, MyTube stores an encrypted OAuth refresh token in its own server-side database (Cloudflare D1), keyed to your MyTube user ID. That token is only used to obtain short-lived access tokens so MyTube can act on YouTube when you explicitly ask it to, such as sending a live-chat message you submit. MyTube never receives or stores your Google password. The connection remains until you disconnect it on your profile page or revoke MyTube's access from your Google Account.

Data stored in your browser

Some information stays only in your browser and is not sent to MyTube's servers:

Preferences

Your browser's local storage keeps small, non-sensitive settings such as the display name used next to your comments.

Profile picture

If you choose a profile picture, it is resized in your browser and saved in your browser's IndexedDB storage, keyed to your account.

Profile pictures are stored locally in your browser and are not uploaded to MyTube's servers. Clearing your browser storage removes them.

Short-lived OAuth security cookie

When you start connecting a YouTube account, MyTube sets a short-lived security cookie named mytube_oauth_state. It contains only the random OAuth state value used to match the connection attempt to the same browser; it does not contain a token, password or account identifier. The cookie is marked HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Lax, is sent only to the OAuth callback path, and expires after about 10 minutes or when the connection attempt completes or fails. The random state value is recorded briefly in MyTube's server-side OAuth state table so the callback can verify it. The cookie is not used for analytics or advertising.

Video content and third-party services

MyTube does not host video files. Videos are discovered through the YouTube Data API and played using YouTube's embedded player. This means the following third parties are involved when you use the site:

YouTube Data API

Video searches, trending lists, related videos and video details are requested from Google's YouTube Data API through MyTube's own server, using a server-side key that is not exposed to your browser.

YouTube embedded player

Videos are played through YouTube's embedded player, and live streams use YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed domain. When a video or its chat is shown, Google/YouTube may receive technical information such as your IP address and may apply its own policies.

YouTube actions you request

When you send a message to a YouTube live chat from MyTube, the text you typed and your YouTube authorization are sent to Google's YouTube Data API so the message can be posted by your own YouTube account. MyTube does not store the message. If you use live-chat restriction help, MyTube may check your connected YouTube account's subscription status for the stream's channel through the YouTube Data API, and only if you explicitly confirm it, MyTube may create a subscription to that channel using your connected YouTube account. On supported MyTube pages, when you explicitly confirm a YouTube account subscription action, MyTube may check your connected YouTube account's subscription status for that channel and create or remove that subscription through the YouTube Data API for your connected YouTube account. MyTube does not auto-subscribe and does not auto-unsubscribe. When you open the YouTube Account Subscriptions section on the Subscriptions page, MyTube may read your connected YouTube account's subscription list from the YouTube Data API to display it to you; this is read-only and does not modify your YouTube subscriptions. MyTube does not store your full YouTube subscription list on its server as part of this feature. When you explicitly click Like or Dislike on a YouTube video and your YouTube account is connected, MyTube sends that rating request (like, dislike, or remove rating) to Google's YouTube Data API for your connected YouTube account, and it may also read your actual rating for that video to display it. MyTube does not silently rate videos on your behalf. YouTube's terms, Community Guidelines and the channel's chat/settings (for example slow mode, subscriber-only, members-only or other restrictions) apply, and YouTube may reject, delay, remove or moderate actions.

Firebase

Sign-in and account data storage are provided by Firebase, a Google service (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Only email/password sign-in is enabled.

Your use of those third-party services is governed by their own terms and privacy policies, not by this page. YouTube embeds and Google/YouTube services can receive technical information about your visit, such as your IP address, and these third-party services may collect and process data in line with their own privacy policies and practices. This page makes no claim about any tracking performed by MyTube's own code.

What MyTube does not do

No passwords

MyTube never stores your account password.

No stored chat messages

Messages you send to a YouTube live chat are not stored by MyTube.

No full subscription lists stored

When you use YouTube live-chat restriction help, MyTube does not store your full YouTube subscription list. Subscription checks and user-confirmed subscription actions are sent to Google/YouTube APIs as you request them.

No selling of personal information

MyTube does not sell users' personal information. This is a policy commitment by the site owner; it is not a technical feature enforced by the site's code.
